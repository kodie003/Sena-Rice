About Sena Rice

From the delta of the Volta River, we bring rice of superior character to your table.

From the delta of the Volta River

Our story

Grown by Ghanaian hands, served worldwide!

Sena Rice is a premium long-grain rice cultivated on our farms along the fertile delta of the Volta River. Every grain is grown with care, harvested at its peak and carefully processed so it reaches your kitchen clean, fluffy and full of natural flavour.

We believe Ghana can feed Ghana. A brand of Direct Meridian Company Limited (DMC), Sena Rice is built on modern mechanised farming, hard-working local farmers and a simple promise: rice of superior character for every home. When you choose Sena Rice, you support local farmers, strengthen rural communities and keep value in the Ghanaian economy.
